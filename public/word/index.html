A word guessing game

Asita's Word

How to play

Guess the hidden word in 6 tries. Each guess must be a real word with 6 letters. Press Enter to check it.

After each guess, the tiles change colour to show how close you were:

  • PLANETP is in the word and in the right place.
  • STREAMT is in the word but in a different place.
  • FORGETG is not in the word anywhere.

A letter can appear more than once. If you guess a letter twice and the word has it only once, only one of your two tiles is coloured.

Daily word: everyone gets the same word, and a new one arrives at midnight. Practice: as many words as you like.
