Asita's Match

All the cards lie face down on the table. Turn over two at a time and find every matching pair. The clock is running, so see how fast you can clear the table.

How to play
  1. Choose a board size. A 4 × 4 board has 16 cards (8 pairs) and an 8 × 8 board has 64 cards (32 pairs). Custom lets you choose any number of rows and columns from 2 to 8.
  2. If the board has an odd number of spaces, as 5 × 5 and 7 × 7 do, the middle space is a free ★ space with no card on it.
  3. Tap a card to turn it over, then tap a second card. If the two match, they stay face up. If not, they turn back over, so try to remember where they were.
  4. The timer starts on your first tap and stops when you find the last pair. Each try at a pair counts as one move.
  5. You can pause at any time. The board is hidden while the game is paused.
  6. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys to move between cards, Enter or Space to turn one over and Esc to pause.
  7. Each round deals objects you didn't have in the last round, where there are enough to choose from. Your best time for every board size is saved on this device.
