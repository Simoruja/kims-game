How to play

Pieces made of four blocks fall into the well one at a time. Slide and turn each piece as it falls. When a row is full from side to side, it clears and the blocks above move down.

Every 10 rows you clear, the level goes up and the pieces fall faster. The game ends when the blocks stack up to the top.

On a phone or tablet

  • Use the buttons, or touch the board: tap to turn, drag left, right or down to move, and flick down to drop.
  • Hold puts the piece aside for later, once per piece. Tapping the Hold box does the same.

On a keyboard

  • ← →Move
  • ↓Move down faster
  • SpaceDrop straight down
  • ↑ XTurn right
  • ZTurn left
  • C ShiftHold
  • P EscPause

Points are multiplied by the level. Clearing rows with pieces in a row adds a combo bonus, and clearing 4 rows twice running (or turning a T piece into a tight gap) scores half as much again. Dropping a piece earns a point or two for each row it falls.
