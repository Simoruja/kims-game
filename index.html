Asita's Game

Study the tray before time runs out. Then see how much you remember. Every round brings new objects: pictures, shapes, numbers, letters and playing cards.

How to play
  1. A tray of objects appears. Memorise as many as you can before the timer runs out, or tap I'm ready.
  2. The tray is covered for a moment.
  3. What's missing? The tray comes back with some objects removed. Pick the missing ones from the choices.
  4. Recall the tray: You see a larger mix of objects. Pick every one that was on the tray.
  5. Custom lets you choose the number of rounds, how many objects you have to guess, the size of the tray and the study time.
  6. After each round, a scorecard shows what you got right, what you got wrong and what you missed. Each correct pick earns a point and each wrong pick loses one. A round never scores below zero.
  7. No object appears on the tray twice in the same game.

Asita's Game is a rendition of Kim's game, which gets its name from Rudyard Kipling's novel Kim (1901). In the novel the young hero is trained to observe this way. Scouts and guides still play it today.
